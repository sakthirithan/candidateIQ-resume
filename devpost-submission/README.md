## 🚀 Quick Submission Instructions for Devpost

1. Open your project edit page on **Devpost**.
2. Copy the elevator pitch from `01-general-info.md`.
3. Copy the full project story from `02-project-story.md` into the **"About the Project"** field.
4. Paste the technology tags from `03-built-with-tags.md` into the **"Built with"** field.
5. Paste the GitHub link (`https://github.com/sakthirithan/ai_interview_platform.git`) into the **"GitHub Repository"** field.
6. Record your 3-minute video following `05-demo-video-script.md`, upload to YouTube, and paste the video link into the **"Video Demo"** field.
7. Capture the 8 screenshots specified in `07-screenshot-plan.md` and upload them to the project gallery.
8. Click **"Submit Project"** before **October 10, 2026, 9:30 PM IST**!
