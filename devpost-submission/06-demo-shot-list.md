# CandidateIQ — Demo Video Production Plan & Shot List

## 1. Technical Preparation & Recording Settings

### Recording Environment
* **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (1080p Full HD), 16:9 aspect ratio
* **Frame Rate**: 30 fps or 60 fps
* **Browser Zoom**: 100% or 110% for crisp typography readability
* **Browser Chrome**: Clean browser window (hide bookmarks bar, extensions, and unrelated tabs)
* **Mouse Cursor**: Large, visible cursor with subtle click highlight
* **Audio Setup**: Clear microphone input, low noise floor, consistent volume level

### Recommended Recording Tools
* **OBS Studio** (Free, Open Source — Full 1080p recording)
* **Screen Studio** / **Loom** (For smooth cursor tracking and automated zoom-ins)
* **CapCut** / **DaVinci Resolve** (For video trimming and audio normalization)

## 4. Post-Production & YouTube Upload Guidance

1. **Trimming**: Cut out loading pauses or browser URL entry delays. Ensure smooth transitions.
2. **Audio Leveling**: Normalize audio to -14 LUFS so speech is clear across all playback devices.
3. **Subtitles**: Optional but recommended (YouTube auto-captions or embedded text highlights).
4. **YouTube Settings**:
   * **Visibility**: Public or Unlisted (Do NOT set to Private; Devpost judges will not be able to view private videos).
   * **Category**: Science & Technology or Education.
   * **Title**: `CandidateIQ — AI-Powered Interview Preparation Platform | ForgeHacks 2026`
   * **Description**: Include links to Devpost page and GitHub repository.
