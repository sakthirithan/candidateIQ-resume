# CandidateIQ — Devpost Screenshot Plan & Asset Guide

## Overview
Devpost permits up to **8 project screenshots** (JPG, PNG, or GIF, max 5 MB each). This document details the exact screens, routes, data requirements, and recommended file names for the submission media assets.

---

## 8 Planned Submission Screenshots

### 01. Candidate Overview Dashboard & Readiness Panel
* **Filename**: `01-candidate-dashboard-readiness.png`
* **Route**: `/dashboard`
* **UI State**: Candidate Overview Dashboard showing compact **Candidate Readiness & Next Steps** panel, 4 summary metric cards, and journey checklist.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Demonstrates the central candidate command center, real-time readiness status, and single source of truth overview.
* **Recommended Composition**: 1920 × 1280 (3:2 ratio) or 1920 × 1080 (16:9).

---

### 02. Resume Intelligence & ATS Quality Parsing
* **Filename**: `02-resume-intelligence-parsing.png`
* **Route**: `/resume-intelligence`
* **UI State**: Resume Intelligence workspace displaying uploaded PDF, extracted technical keywords, and ATS criteria quality score breakdown.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Proves automated PDF text parsing, skill keyword extraction, and structural ATS quality feedback.

---

### 03. Target Job Requisition Compatibility Match
* **Filename**: `03-target-job-compatibility-match.png`
* **Route**: `/jobs`
* **UI State**: Job Discovery desk showing target job requisition details, matching skill breakdown (Matching vs Required), and application submission controls.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Highlights role-specific skill gap matching before taking an interview.

---

### 04. Interactive AI Mock Interview Room
* **Filename**: `04-ai-mock-interview-room.png`
* **Route**: `/interview`
* **UI State**: AI Mock Interview Room displaying dynamic role-tailored technical questions, countdown timer, and answer response area.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Demonstrates live interactive mock interview execution derived from candidate resume and job requirements.

---

### 05. Objective 6-Metric Answer Evaluation Scorecard
* **Filename**: `05-interview-evaluation-analytics.png`
* **Route**: `/mock-interview`
* **UI State**: Multi-dimensional evaluation scorecard displaying 6-metric breakdown (Technical, Depth, Problem Solving, Communication, Relevance, Behavioural) and radar chart.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Highlights objective, non-biased AI response scoring and detailed question-by-question analytics.

---

### 06. Skill Matrix & Evidence Triangulation
* **Filename**: `06-skill-matrix-triangulation.png`
* **Route**: `/skills`
* **UI State**: Interactive Skill Matrix displaying skill proficiency categories, multi-source evidence tags (*Supported*, *Partial*, *Untested*), and confidence scores.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Illustrates evidence-backed skill classification comparing resume claims against interview performance.

---

### 07. Actionable Improvement Activities Hub
* **Filename**: `07-improvement-activities-practice.png`
* **Route**: `/activities`
* **UI State**: Improvement Activities Hub displaying weakness-derived practice tasks, baseline vs. latest progress, and interactive practice controls.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Demonstrates how CandidateIQ converts interview evaluation feedback directly into actionable learning tasks.

---

### 08. Candidate Journey Progression & Attempt Tracking
* **Filename**: `08-candidate-journey-progression.png`
* **Route**: `/interview-journey`
* **UI State**: Interview Journey timeline showing multi-attempt score progression and milestone completion checklist across sessions.
* **Demonstration Purpose**: Proves long-term candidate tracking and continuous learning loop (Assess -> Diagnose -> Improve -> Reassess).
